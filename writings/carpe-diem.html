Carpe Diem

Mateo Rubiera

A man slaves away.
Every day.
He builds the church so the people can pray.
All of their sins away.

He builds by himself.
None offer help.
“One day He will see.
One day He will love me.”

But not today.
Today He breaks your back.
In they come to pray.
But prayers for you they lack.

For another has gotten hurt.
More popular than you.
“There is nothing more I can do.
It is not my back that hurts!”

One day the church outlived him.
The next he had been forgotten.
On his tombstone all it read:
“CARPE DIEM”
